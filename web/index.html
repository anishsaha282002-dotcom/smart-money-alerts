Three grades

Shown side by side. Never blended into one score.

A single number hides what it is made of. Each alert shows three separate reads, so you can see when a great wallet is buying a fading theme, or a hyped coin has nobody credible behind it.

Wallet grade, A to D

Built only from closed trades in the last 30 days.

  • Win rate pulled toward 50% when the sample is small
  • Under 5 closed trades can never grade above C
  • Penalties for bots, bundles, snipers, wash trading, fresh-funded clusters

Caller grade

Who on X mentioned the coin, and what their past calls did next.

  • Price 15 minutes, 1 hour and 24 hours after each call
  • Fewer than 3 aged calls shows as “unrated”
  • Each caller listed by name with their own numbers

How it works

Free data in. Plain English out.

Watch

Reads public on-chain trades on Solana and BNB Chain from free APIs. No wallet connection, ever.

Grade

Grades the wallet, the callers and the narrative separately, with fixed rules you can read in the code.

Tell

Writes a short story and sends it to the console, Telegram, Discord and this site.

Check

Records every alert in a hash-chained ledger and prices it again at 15m, 1h, 4h and 24h.

# try the whole thing in three commands, no keys needed
