Public track record

Every alert. Including the ones that lost.

Each alert is written to an append-only ledger before it is sent, then priced again at 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours and 24 hours. Nothing is removed. Results are measured from the price when the alert went out, not from the wallet's better entry.

By wallet grade

Did the better grades do better?

For each checkpoint: the share of alerts trading above the alert-time price, how many that is, and the median move. The small tick marks 50%. Coins nobody traded any more are counted as misses.

Paper run

Running total of a fixed stake into every alert, sold one hour later. Not a strategy, just a number anyone can check against the ledger.

Could you still copy it?

For every alert, what was left 5 minutes after the wallet bought, measured to the 4-hour peak.

These are best cases: nobody sells the exact peak.

Verify it yourself

Do not trust this page. Check it.

Every ledger line contains the SHA-256 hash of the line before it. Change, delete or insert any earlier line and the chain breaks. Your browser can recompute all of it right now, with nothing sent anywhere.
